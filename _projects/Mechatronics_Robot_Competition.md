For my Mechatronics Class, MAE 3780, our final project was to work in group to design and build a robot that work work autonomously in a competition. This competition focused on one on one rounds, where two robots were placed in an arena and worked autonomously to collect the most amount of blocks in comparison to the other robot in a 60 second period.
